# FAITH Protocol — One Page Investor Memo

## What FAITH Is

FAITH Protocol is PCS-regulated credit and treasury infrastructure for autonomous onchain economies on MegaETH.

FAITH is not being built as a speculative token project or a simple lending app. It is being designed as a capital operating system where programmable credit, treasury resilience, protocol-level risk intelligence, and real-time execution work together to support disciplined economic growth.

Core line:

**Humans build. PCS regulates. Treasury protects. MegaETH executes. The economy grows.**

## Why Now

Onchain economies are becoming faster, more autonomous, and more complex. Stablecoins, RWAs, AI agents, gaming economies, marketplaces, and high-performance chains all need stronger risk-aware credit and treasury systems.

Most DeFi lending infrastructure remains reactive. It may show user-level collateral ratios, health factors, and liquidation prices, but it often does not provide clear protocol-level interpretation of treasury coverage, liquidation pressure, oracle shocks, borrow utilization, and systemic risk.

FAITH is built around the belief that credit expansion should be balanced by machine-readable risk controls and treasury discipline.

## Differentiator

The core differentiator is PCS, the Protocol Control System.

PCS observes protocol state, scores systemic risk, explains risk drivers, simulates stress scenarios, and recommends protocol-level responses.

PCS does not advise users. PCS advises the protocol.

During the testnet MVP phase, FAITH publicly describes the PCS risk categories at a high level, including oracle risk, vault health risk, liquidation pressure, borrow utilization, and treasury coverage. Detailed scoring parameters, weighting logic, treasury strategy, and future capital-routing mechanics remain internal.

## Why MegaETH

FAITH benefits from real-time execution, fast state awareness, and high-performance financial interactions.

MegaETH is a strong environment for building protocol-level credit and risk infrastructure because FAITH’s system is designed to react quickly to collateral changes, liquidation pressure, treasury conditions, and protocol state.
