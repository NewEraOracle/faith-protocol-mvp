# UtopiaByFaith Whitepaper

## A Play-to-Originate RPG Economy Powered by FAITH and USDm

**Version:** Updated Vision Draft  
**Project:** UtopiaByFaith  
**Positioning:** Gameplay-first virtual world economy  
**Core Economic Line:** Players use FAITH to create value. Players use USDm to trade value.

---

## Important Notice

This document is a conceptual and gameplay-economy vision paper for UtopiaByFaith.

It is not financial advice, legal advice, investment advice, or an offer to sell securities.

UtopiaByFaith should be positioned as a gameplay-first virtual world, not as a guaranteed income product, investment opportunity, or token speculation system.

Any blockchain, NFT, marketplace, token, or real-money-related feature must be developed with legal review, player protection, compliance analysis, security audits, and clear risk disclosures.

UtopiaByFaith is designed around gameplay, worldbuilding, artifact creation, player ownership, and optional marketplace commerce.

---

## 1. Abstract

UtopiaByFaith is a gameplay-first virtual world and RPG economy where players explore, fight, forge, own, and trade gameplay-originated artifacts.

The project is built around a healthier alternative to old play-to-earn models:

**Play-to-Originate.**

In UtopiaByFaith, players do not simply farm tokens. They enter a world, choose an identity, fight through dungeons, gather resources, interact with NPCs, forge artifacts, and create rare gameplay items.

These artifacts can be equipped, upgraded, kept, displayed, or optionally traded through a USDm-powered marketplace.

The updated economic model separates the role of FAITH and USDm clearly:

- **FAITH = creation, forging, progression, access, identity**
- **USDm = trading, pricing, marketplace commerce, settlement**

Core loop:

**Play ? Forge with FAITH ? Own artifact ? Trade in USDm ? Progress ? Forge again**

## 5. Play-to-Originate

Play-to-Originate is the core economic philosophy of UtopiaByFaith.

Traditional play-to-earn models often focus on token emissions, repetitive farming, extractive incentives, and unsustainable rewards.

UtopiaByFaith takes a different direction.

Instead of paying players to grind tokens, the game lets players originate scarce digital artifacts through gameplay.

Players may originate value by:

- Completing quests
- Defeating bosses
- Gathering resources
- Unlocking recipes
- Using FAITH at the forge
- Minting randomized artifacts
- Upgrading rare items
- Discovering faction relics
- Participating in seasonal events

Important distinction:

- **Play-to-Earn = play to extract tokens**
- **Play-to-Originate = play to create rare gameplay artifacts**

Players are not promised profit. They are given a world where gameplay can produce scarce digital items with optional marketplace utility.

## 6. World Narrative

UtopiaByFaith takes place in the fallen kingdom of Utopia.

The kingdom has been corrupted by the Empire of Devils. The world is divided between forces of Light and Darkness, each fighting for control, relics, caves, resources, and spiritual dominion.

Players enter the world as champions who must choose their allegiance.

### Light Faction

The Light faction seeks to cleanse corrupted caves, protect sacred relics, and restore divine order to Utopia.

Possible Light patrons:

- Michael
- Gabriel
- Other celestial patrons in future expansions

The Light path may focus on purification, protection, healing, holy damage, relic restoration, defensive buffs, and divine artifacts.

### Dark Faction

The Dark faction seeks to defend, corrupt, or dominate caves, relics, and forbidden powers.

Possible Dark patrons:

- Baal
- Beelzebub
- Other fallen patrons in future expansions

The Dark path may focus on corruption, domination, curses, shadow damage, forbidden relics, aggressive buffs, and demonic artifacts.

## 8. Core Economy

The updated UtopiaByFaith economy has two clear economic rails:

**FAITH powers creation.**  
**USDm powers commerce.**

This separation is critical.

FAITH is not used as the main trading currency of the marketplace. FAITH is used to originate, forge, upgrade, and access gameplay systems.

USDm is used for stable marketplace pricing and player-to-player commerce.

## 11. Dual-Currency Model

The dual-currency model is one of UtopiaByFaith's most important design principles.

- **FAITH = creation, progression, access, identity**
- **USDm = commerce, pricing, trade, settlement**

Golden rule:

**Players use FAITH to create value. Players use USDm to trade value.**

Simple loop:

**Player obtains FAITH ? Player uses FAITH to forge artifact ? Artifact receives randomized rarity ? Player equips, upgrades, keeps, or sells artifact ? Marketplace sale happens in USDm ? Player uses FAITH again for future creation and progression**

## 14. Forge and Wizard NPC System

The forge or wizard NPC is the main interface for artifact creation.

Forge flow:

1. Player visits forge or wizard NPC
2. Player selects artifact category
3. Player selects artifact type
4. Player reviews FAITH cost
5. Player confirms forge attempt
6. System generates artifact
7. Artifact receives rarity and stats
8. Artifact appears in inventory
9. Player chooses next action

After creating an artifact, the player may equip it, keep it, upgrade it, display it, combine it, list it on the marketplace, or sell it for USDm.
