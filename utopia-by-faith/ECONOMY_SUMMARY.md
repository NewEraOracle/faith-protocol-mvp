# UtopiaByFaith Economy Summary

## Core Economic Line

**Players use FAITH to create value. Players use USDm to trade value.**

UtopiaByFaith is a gameplay-first RPG economy built around play-to-originate, not play-to-earn.

The goal is to let players create rare gameplay-originated artifacts through exploration, combat, quests, resources, and forging.

---

## Main Loop

Play -> Forge with FAITH -> Own artifact -> Equip, upgrade, keep, or list -> Trade in USDm -> Progress -> Forge again

---

## Token Roles

### FAITH

FAITH is used for creation and progression.

Players may use FAITH for:

- Artifact minting
- Forge attempts
- Weapon creation
- Armor creation
- Shield creation
- Relic creation
- Artifact upgrades
- Patron attunement
- Faction progression
- Elite access
- Rare forge tiers

### USDm

USDm is used for commerce and marketplace settlement.

Players may use USDm for:

- Marketplace buying
- Marketplace selling
- Player-to-player trades
- Stable pricing
- Merchant purchases
- Event entries
- Commerce settlement

---

## Golden Rule

FAITH creates value.

USDm trades value.

Gameplay originates value.

---

## Why This Matters

UtopiaByFaith avoids the old play-to-earn model where users farm tokens for extraction.

Instead, players originate artifacts through gameplay.

The economy should support the world, not replace the game.

---

## Product Separation

UtopiaByFaith remains separate from FAITH Protocol during the current stage.

FAITH Protocol is the institutional credit, treasury, PCS, and RWA infrastructure layer.

UtopiaByFaith is the gameplay, artifact, forge, inventory, and marketplace economy layer.

Future ecosystem connection may happen later, but each product must prove its own core loop first.
