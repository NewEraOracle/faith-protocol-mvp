# UtopiaByFaith Roadmap

## Execution Principle

UtopiaByFaith should be built gameplay-first.

The goal is to prove the game loop, artifact creation loop, and USDm marketplace loop before adding full blockchain complexity.

Core loop:

Play -> Forge with FAITH -> Own artifact -> Trade in USDm -> Progress

## Important Rule

UtopiaByFaith should remain separate from FAITH Protocol during the current stage.

FAITH Protocol = institutional credit, treasury, PCS, RWA bridge, capital infrastructure.

UtopiaByFaith = gameplay world, artifact economy, FAITH minting, USDm marketplace.

Future ecosystem connection may happen later, after each product proves its own core loop.
