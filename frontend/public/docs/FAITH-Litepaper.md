# FAITH Monetary Protocol Litepaper

## A Human-Built, PCS-Regulated Economic Operating System

### 1. Vision

FAITH Monetary Protocol is building a machine-regulated economic operating system for disciplined digital growth.

The protocol is designed around a simple principle:

**Humans build. PCS regulates. Treasury protects. MegaETH executes. The economy grows.**

FAITH is not only a lending interface. It is an infrastructure layer for programmable credit, treasury resilience, risk regulation, and productive economic activity.

The long-term objective is to create an economy where growth does not depend only on speculation, emissions, or market hype, but on useful activity, controlled credit expansion, and protocol-level protection.

---

### 2. The Problem

Many digital economies grow quickly but become fragile because they rely too heavily on speculation, uncontrolled incentives, or unstable liquidity.

Common problems include:

- Credit systems expanding faster than reserves.
- Treasury systems that do not actively protect users or the protocol.
- Governance decisions made without real-time risk intelligence.
- Token economies that depend mainly on price speculation instead of productive utility.
- Poor visibility into liquidation pressure, reserve coverage, and systemic stress.

FAITH is designed to address these problems by connecting credit, treasury, and risk regulation into one coordinated system.

---

### 3. The FAITH Economic Operating System

FAITH connects three major layers:

**Credit Layer**  
Vault-based credit infrastructure where collateral can support programmable credit creation.

**Treasury Layer**  
A reserve and capital protection layer designed to strengthen protocol resilience over time.

**PCS Risk Layer**  
The Protocol Control System monitors system health, stress conditions, credit expansion, treasury coverage, and liquidation pressure.

Together, these layers create the foundation for a disciplined on-chain economy.

FAITH is designed to grow, but not blindly. Credit expansion should be balanced by treasury strength and PCS risk control.

---

### 4. The Three-Asset Model

FAITH separates the economy into three distinct roles.

#### FXMP — Growth and Coordination Token

FXMP is the public growth token of Faith Monetary Protocol.

It is designed for:

- Ecosystem exposure
- Protocol coordination
- Future governance direction
- Access and participation
- Growth alignment

FXMP represents the more public and growth-oriented side of the FAITH economy.

#### FUSD — Internal Credit Asset

FUSD is the protocol internal credit asset.

It is designed for:

- Borrowing
- Repayments
- Credit circulation
- Vault-based activity
- Productive on-chain economic movement

FUSD is not positioned as a speculative growth token. Its purpose is to move credit inside the system.

#### FXTC — Treasury Capital Token

FXTC is the treasury-aligned capital layer.

It is designed for long-term participants who want exposure to the development and treasury strength of Faith Monetary Protocol.

FXTC is intended to represent a more stable and controlled participation layer than FXMP. It is connected to reserve strength, treasury resilience, and long-term protocol development.

FXTC is not publicly marketed as guaranteed yield, guaranteed profit, guaranteed redemption, or guaranteed principal protection.

---

### 5. PCS Risk Regulation

PCS stands for Protocol Control System.

PCS is designed to act as the protocol risk intelligence layer.

It can monitor:

- Vault health
- Credit utilization
- Treasury coverage
- Collateral stress
- Liquidation pressure
- Reserve strength
- Emergency risk conditions

PCS does not replace governance. It improves governance by giving the protocol better risk awareness.

The purpose of PCS is to help FAITH grow without losing discipline.

Exact PCS formulas, thresholds, scoring models, and private risk logic are not disclosed in the public litepaper.

---

### 6. Treasury Protection

The treasury is designed to protect the long-term strength of the FAITH economy.

Instead of relying only on emissions or speculative token demand, FAITH aims to build treasury resilience through disciplined economic activity.

The treasury can support:

- Reserve protection
- Risk buffers
- Long-term solvency
- Controlled growth
- Future ecosystem development
- Protocol defense during stress

The treasury is not presented as a guarantee. It is a protection and resilience layer that must evolve with legal review, audits, governance, and risk controls.

---

### 7. MegaETH Execution

FAITH is designed to be MegaETH-native.

MegaETH provides the execution environment for real-time protocol activity, fast state updates, low-latency interactions, and high-performance financial infrastructure.

This matters because a machine-regulated economy needs fast visibility.

PCS becomes more powerful when it can monitor and react to live protocol conditions quickly.

MegaETH gives FAITH the foundation to build real-time credit, treasury, and risk infrastructure.

---

### 8. Build and Ecosystem Path

FAITH is not only a protocol for users. It is also a platform for builders.

The Build layer is designed for:

- MegaETH ecosystem builders
- RWA and productive infrastructure projects
- DeFi credit and liquidity developers
- Treasury and risk analytics teams
- Earn and yield strategists
- AI agents and automation builders
- Strategic protocol partners

Builders can eventually create applications around credit flows, vault infrastructure, treasury analytics, PCS signals, simulations, and productive economic activity.

The goal is to grow FAITH into an ecosystem, not just a single product.

### 10. Legal and Compliance Boundary

FAITH is an early-stage protocol concept and testnet MVP.

Any future launch involving real capital, treasury certificates, RWAs, yield strategies, or investment-like participation requires legal review, compliance review, audits, risk assessment, and jurisdiction-specific analysis.

Public materials should not be interpreted as:

- Financial advice
- Investment advice
- A guarantee of returns
- A guarantee of redemption
- A public securities offering
- A live yield product
- A promise of future token value

FAITH public litepaper is for technical, ecosystem, and educational communication only.
