## 1. Abstract

FAITH Protocol is a MegaETH-native institutional financial ecosystem designed to bridge real-world assets and productive infrastructure into an autonomous digital economy.

The protocol begins with on-chain vaults, collateralized credit, stable credit circulation, treasury reserves, oracle-based valuation, liquidation testing, and PCS risk monitoring.

Core thesis:

Humans build real-world value.  
FAITH transforms that value into programmable credit.  
PCS and technology monitor, regulate, and protect the digital economy.  
Treasury strength compounds the system.  
Capital flows back toward productive growth.

## 2. Core Vision

FAITH Protocol is a human-built, machine-regulated capital operating system for productive economies.

## 7. Product Separation

FAITH Protocol and UtopiaByFaith must remain separate during the current stage.

FAITH Protocol is focused on institutional credit, treasury, PCS, risk systems, oracle shocks, RWA bridge infrastructure, and future capital routing.

UtopiaByFaith is focused on gameplay, artifact forging, FAITH minting utility, USDm marketplace commerce, player inventory, and digital artifacts.

Both may connect later under a broader FAITH ecosystem, but each product must prove its own core loop first.
