# FAITH Protocol Demo Script

## Goal

This is a 60-90 second walkthrough script for FAITH Protocol.

The goal is to show that FAITH is not only a lending MVP.

FAITH is MegaETH-native credit, treasury, and risk infrastructure with PCS as the protocol risk and parameter engine.

---

## 60-90 Second Demo Flow

### 1. Open Homepage

Narration:

FAITH Protocol is a MegaETH-native capital operating system for credit, treasury, and risk infrastructure.

The long-term vision is human-built, machine-regulated finance.

Humans build productive value. PCS regulates protocol risk. Treasury protects the system.

---

### 2. Open Dashboard

Narration:

This is the live MegaETH testnet dashboard.

It demonstrates vaults, test collateral, stable credit, oracle shock simulation, liquidation risk, treasury health, and PCS monitoring.

---

### 3. Tester Setup

Show:

- Open wallet
- Open MegaETH Faucet
- Claim tFAITH

Narration:

The demo flow is designed for testers.

Users can open the MegaETH faucet directly, get testnet gas, connect MetaMask, and claim tFAITH test collateral.

For mobile, the best option is currently MetaMask mobile browser.

---

### 4. Protocol Overview

Show:

- PCS Status
- Treasury Health
- Total Collateral
- Total Debt
- Oracle Price

Narration:

The protocol overview shows live system conditions.

PCS now calculates a real risk score from protocol data.

Treasury Health is connected to PCS coverage logic.

---

### 5. PCS Parameter Engine

Show:

- System Risk Score
- Oracle Risk
- Treasury Coverage
- Liquidation Pressure
- Suggested Parameter Response
- Risk Rationale

Narration:

This is the key upgrade.

PCS is not a financial advisor.

PCS is a protocol risk and parameter engine.

It observes protocol conditions, calculates risk, suggests protocol-level responses, and explains the rationale.

---

### 6. PCS Risk Parameter Panel

Show:

- Current LTV
- Suggested LTV
- Liquidation Threshold
- Reserve Target
- Borrow Utilization
- Debt Coverage
- Emergency Mode

Narration:

PCS translates risk into protocol parameter responses.

For example, if oracle risk increases, PCS can suggest tightening risk parameters or reducing LTV.

This is what turns FAITH from a lending dashboard into machine-regulated credit infrastructure.

---

### 7. PCS Stress Simulator

Show:

- -10% shock
- -20% shock
- -30% shock

Narration:

PCS also projects stress scenarios.

If collateral drops further, PCS estimates projected risk level, projected risk score, and suggested protocol response.

This gives FAITH a risk command center layer.

---

### 8. Oracle Shock Simulator

Show:

- Simulate price shock
- Watch risk change

Narration:

The oracle shock simulator lets testers stress the protocol and observe how PCS, treasury coverage, and liquidation risk respond.

---

### 9. Activity Feed

Show:

- Collateral Deposited
- Stable Credit Borrowed
- Debt Repaid
- Oracle Shock Recorded
- Liquidation Executed

Narration:

The activity feed shows live on-chain events from the credit, collateral, oracle, liquidation, and PCS risk flow.

---

### 10. Future Capital Routing

Narration:

Future capital routing is not live investment infrastructure.

It remains a research phase that would require legal structures, compliance, audits, partners, reporting, and risk controls.

---

## Closing Statement

FAITH is not just a lending MVP.

FAITH is becoming MegaETH-native machine-regulated credit infrastructure.

The next milestone is moving PCS deeper into smart contract, backend, and indexer logic so it can monitor more vaults, track history, and support protocol-level risk governance.

---

## Short Pitch Version

FAITH Protocol is building MegaETH-native institutional credit, treasury, and risk infrastructure.

The signature feature is PCS, a protocol risk and parameter engine.

PCS calculates a 0-100 risk score, suggests parameter responses, explains risk rationale, and simulates collateral shocks.

That is what moves FAITH beyond a normal DeFi lending dashboard and toward machine-regulated credit infrastructure.
