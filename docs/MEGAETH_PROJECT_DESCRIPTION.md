# MegaETH Project Description

## Project Name

FAITH Protocol

---

## One-Liner

FAITH Protocol is building MegaETH-native institutional credit, treasury, and risk infrastructure for an autonomous digital economy.

---

## Short Description

FAITH Protocol is a testnet MVP on MegaETH focused on collateralized credit, vault health, oracle shock simulation, liquidation risk, treasury monitoring, and PCS risk controls.

The protocol is designed to evolve into a human-built, machine-regulated capital operating system for productive economies.

---

## Why MegaETH

FAITH is built for real-time financial infrastructure.

MegaETH is important because FAITH depends on:

- Real-time collateral monitoring
- Fast oracle shock response
- Rapid liquidation testing
- Continuous risk monitoring
- Low-latency credit activity
- Institutional-grade on-chain feedback loops

The thesis is simple:

Credit, risk, treasury, and liquidation systems should react at internet speed.

MegaETH makes that direction possible.

---

## Current MVP

The current FAITH Protocol MVP demonstrates:

- tFAITH collateral
- tfUSD stable credit borrowing
- Vault creation
- Borrow and repay flow
- Oracle shock simulation
- Liquidation risk monitoring
- PCS Status module
- Treasury Health module
- Liquidation Risk module
- Human-Built / Machine-Regulated Economy section
- Future Capital Routing roadmap

Core demo flow:

Deposit collateral -> Borrow tfUSD -> Simulate oracle shock -> Monitor vault health -> Trigger liquidation risk -> Protect protocol solvency

---

## Signature Feature: PCS

PCS means Parameter Control System.

PCS is the risk monitoring layer of FAITH Protocol.

PCS monitors:

- Vault health
- Oracle risk
- Treasury coverage
- Borrow utilization
- Liquidation pressure
- System solvency

Long term, PCS may evolve toward autonomous parameter recommendations or adjustments.

PCS is what makes FAITH more than a lending demo.

It turns FAITH into risk-regulated capital infrastructure.

---

## Future Direction

FAITH Protocol is designed to evolve from a testnet credit MVP into institutional credit, treasury, risk, and future capital-routing infrastructure.

Future research areas include:

- Real estate capital pools
- Energy infrastructure pools
- Venture pools
- Technology infrastructure pools
- Treasury reserve pools
- PCS-monitored capital routing

These are future research phases only.

Any real-world asset integration requires legal structure, compliance review, audited reporting, partners, and risk controls.

---

## Product Separation

FAITH Protocol remains separate from UtopiaByFaith during the current stage.

FAITH Protocol = institutional credit, treasury, PCS, RWA bridge, capital infrastructure.

UtopiaByFaith = gameplay-first RPG economy, artifact forging, FAITH minting utility, and USDm marketplace commerce.

Future ecosystem connection may happen later, after each product proves its own core loop.
