# FAITH Ecosystem Map

## High-Level Ecosystem Flow

The FAITH ecosystem should be understood as two separate products today, with a future connection layer later.

Current separation:

- FAITH Protocol = institutional credit, treasury, PCS, RWA bridge, and capital infrastructure.
- UtopiaByFaith = gameplay-first RPG economy, artifact forging, FAITH utility, and USDm marketplace commerce.

Future connection should happen only after each product proves its own core loop.

---

## Master Ecosystem Loop

Real-World Infrastructure
-> FAITH Protocol
-> PCS / Risk Engine
-> Treasury + Capital Routing
-> UtopiaByFaith
-> FAITH Minting + USDm Marketplace
-> Fees / Activity / Treasury Growth
-> Reinvestment into infrastructure and ecosystem growth

---

## Layer 1 — Real-World Infrastructure

Humans build productive value through:

- Real estate
- Energy infrastructure
- Ventures
- Businesses
- Technology infrastructure
- AI infrastructure
- Communities
- Productive assets

This is the human-built layer.

---

## Layer 2 — FAITH Protocol

FAITH Protocol transforms productive value into programmable credit infrastructure.

Core modules:

- Vaults
- Collateral
- Stable credit
- Borrow / repay flow
- Oracle data
- Liquidation risk
- Treasury reserves
- PCS monitoring

This is the institutional finance layer.

---

## Layer 3 — PCS / Risk Engine

PCS means Parameter Control System.

PCS monitors:

- Vault health
- Oracle risk
- Treasury coverage
- Borrow utilization
- Liquidation pressure
- System solvency
- Future capital pool exposure

PCS is the machine-regulated intelligence layer.

---

## Layer 4 — Treasury + Capital Routing

The treasury is the reserve engine.

Future capital routing may support:

- Real estate pools
- Energy pools
- Venture pools
- Technology infrastructure pools
- Business financing pools
- Treasury reserve pools

This remains a future research phase requiring legal structure, compliance review, audits, partners, reporting, and risk controls.

---

## Layer 5 — UtopiaByFaith

UtopiaByFaith is the gameplay-first economy layer.

Players explore, fight, gather resources, forge artifacts, own items, and optionally trade them.

Core loop:

Play -> Forge with FAITH -> Own artifact -> Trade in USDm -> Progress

---

## Layer 6 — FAITH Minting + USDm Marketplace

UtopiaByFaith uses a dual-currency model:

- FAITH = creation, forging, upgrades, progression, access, identity
- USDm = trading, pricing, marketplace commerce, settlement

Golden rule:

FAITH creates value.

USDm trades value.

Gameplay originates value.

---

## Layer 7 — Fees / Activity / Treasury Growth

Future ecosystem activity may create:

- Protocol usage
- Marketplace activity
- Treasury flows
- Risk reserve growth
- Capital routing capacity
- Ecosystem sustainability

No part of this should be framed as guaranteed yield or passive income.

---

## Layer 8 — Reinvestment Loop

The long-term vision is:

Productive capital enters the ecosystem.

FAITH organizes and monitors credit.

PCS regulates risk.

Treasury strength grows.

Capital can eventually route back toward productive infrastructure.

UtopiaByFaith gives people a world to experience FAITH utility.

---

## Important Separation Rule

Do not merge FAITH Protocol and UtopiaByFaith too early.

Current stage:

FAITH Protocol must prove institutional credit, risk, treasury, PCS, and MegaETH-native infrastructure.

UtopiaByFaith must prove gameplay, forging, artifact ownership, and USDm marketplace commerce.

Future ecosystem connection should come later, only after both products are strong independently.
